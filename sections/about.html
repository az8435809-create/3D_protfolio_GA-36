My story

From a Small Room in Karachi to the Digital Mountains.

It started with a laptop, a slow connection and a lot of curiosity in a small room in Karachi. I wanted to know how the websites I loved were put together, so I opened the code and kept going. Every broken layout taught me something, and every finished page made me want to build a bigger one.

Today I study at NED University of Engineering & Technology and build websites, interfaces and interactive 3D experiences for real people. I care about the details most visitors never notice: clean structure, fast loading, smooth motion and a design that feels calm and easy to use.
