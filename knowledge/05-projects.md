# Karachi Cyberpunk: Neon Rickshaw (Game Development)
A stylized 3D action-runner set in a futuristic Saddar and Clifton, Karachi, with custom Niagara VFX.
How it was built: modular Karachi street props modeled in Blender, 4K PBR textures baked in Substance Painter, vehicle physics and neon lighting scripted in Unreal Engine 5.
Tools: Unreal Engine 5, Blender, Substance Painter.

# Rigged Stylized 3D Avatar (3D Character)
A game-ready character with a full-body armature, facial blendshapes and real-time WebGL shaders.
How it was built: sculpted a high-poly mesh, retopologized to clean quad topology (about 18k triangles) and exported rigged GLB animations for Three.js and Unity.
Tools: Blender, IK/FK rig, GLB / glTF 2.0.

# 60s Kinetic VFX Showreel (Motion Design)
A promotional reel combining 3D camera tracking, kinetic typography and sound design.
How it was built: Blender multi-pass EXR renders composited in After Effects with speed-ramped graph-editor curves and licensed audio.
Tools: After Effects, Premiere Pro, 3D render passes.

# Interactive 3D Portfolio (Web Experience)
A scroll-choreographed 3D portfolio with a physics tech-stack and a grounded AI assistant.
How it was built: Three.js real-time lighting, TF-IDF cosine-similarity retrieval with source citations, and an email contact flow.
Tools: Three.js, Node.js, RAG.

# Mohatta Palace VR Tour (VR Experience)
A VR cultural-heritage walkthrough of Karachi's Mohatta Palace that won 1st Runner-Up at the Karachi Tech & Digital Arts Fest Game & VR Showcase (2024).
How it was built: recreated the domes and courtyard lighting in Blender and Unity XR with spatial audio; textures in Substance Painter.
Tools: Unity XR, Blender, C#.

# AI NPC Lore & Dialogue Engine (AI Engineering)
A context-aware NPC dialogue system grounded in verified world-lore documents.
How it was built: retrieves relevant quest-lore chunks by vector similarity (embeddings + RAG) so NPCs answer from the game's real lore instead of making things up.
Tools: embeddings, RAG, Unity C#.
