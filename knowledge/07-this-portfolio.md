# The 3D avatar
The portfolio includes "Meet the 3D Me": a real-time 3D version of Ali's mascot (brown checkered suit, white shirt, silver leaf pin, brown hair, big brown eyes, one hand in his pocket). It is modeled and textured, rigged with an 18-bone skeleton, has 4 expression morph targets (blink left, blink right, smile, surprised) and 5 animations (idle, wave, nod, surprised, celebrate), and is exported as a GLB (glTF 2.0) file. Visitors can rotate it, and its head follows the cursor. A Blender script rebuilds the same character in Blender.

# The showreel video
A 46-second promotional video (1920×1080, 30 fps) introduces Ali: his name and introduction, skills, six projects, the 3D avatar, achievements, and his contact email, with an original soundtrack and captions. An After Effects script rebuilds the video as an editable After Effects project.

# Site features
Dark and light themes, interactive 3D objects that respond to the mouse, project filtering, search, sorting and drag-to-reorder, privacy-friendly analytics, accessibility settings (text size, reduce motion, high contrast, underline links), this AI chatbot, and a contact form.
