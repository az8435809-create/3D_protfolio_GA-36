# Who is Ali Zain
Ali Zain is a final-year undergraduate student and a web developer, UI designer and 3D artist from Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan.
He is studying for a B.S. in Gaming and Animation at NED University of Engineering & Technology (NEDUET) in Karachi.
His portfolio tagline is "I build digital experiences that reach new heights." He turns ideas into clean, fast and beautiful websites, and he bridges artistic storytelling with real-time engineering: 3D characters, game engines, motion graphics and AI-powered web platforms.

# Location and availability
Ali is based in Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan. He is open to freelance and full-time work, available in Karachi or remotely worldwide.
He is open to Game Art, 3D Animation, Interactive Web, web development and UI/UX design roles, and he is aiming to work with international clients.

# His story
It started with a laptop, a slow connection and a lot of curiosity in a small room in Karachi. He wanted to know how the websites he loved were put together, so he opened the code and kept going. Every broken layout taught him something, and every finished page made him want to build a bigger one.
He cares about details most visitors never notice: clean structure, fast loading, smooth motion and calm, easy-to-use design.

# Working principles
Clean Code: readable, structured code that is easy to grow. Pixel Perfect: designs built exactly as drawn. Fast Performance: light pages that load quickly. SEO Friendly: semantic markup that search engines can read.
